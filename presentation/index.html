How it works

One loop, any arm

The one contract every arm speaks

execute(task) → ok | failed | timeout | arm_down
Real, simulated and fake arms all implement it.

06 / 08
Speed-up is never N times. The finish time can't beat the work split across arms, the longest chain of ordered steps, or the busiest shared area. We compute that bound live and say which one binds. Here: 12 minutes of work, a 5-minute chain, so the third arm helps less and the fourth not at all. (~35 s)
